ABADDON OFFICIAL SITE v6.5.2 UPLOAD GUIDE

Upload the CONTENTS of the ZIP so index.html is at the deployment root.
Required root entries: index.html, style.css, script.js, config.js, commands.html, updates.html, assets/.
