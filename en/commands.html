🎵 ABADDON Music · Music commands

Music is played by the dedicated ABADDON Music bot. Join a voice channel and type !music play song name. repeat takes off/one/all, autoplay on keeps playing similar songs after the queue ends, and idle (1-60 min) sets how long the bot waits before leaving.
