!cardgames

Open the authentic 16-mode card-game menu. Invite ABADDON when no survivor opponent is available.

!abaddongames 10000

Choose four quick mini-games or 16 authentic card modes. Card modes start real sessions instead of instant comparison.

!inviteabaddon seotda 10000

Start an authentic Seotda session directly against ABADDON. Go-Stop automatically fills two ABADDON seats.

!seotda 10000

Play Seotda through first-card betting, second-card betting, hand reveal, and Gusa redeals.

!cardrules

Review the actual turn, betting, draw, reveal, and scoring flow for all 16 card modes.

!gameplayaudit

Check authentic session registration, debt-wallet storage, uncapped settlement, Seotda, Go-Stop, and ABADDON AI wiring.

!poker 10000

Five-card draw: opening betting, exchange up to three cards, final betting, then showdown.

!texasholdem 10000

Post small and big blinds, then play preflop, flop, turn, and river betting streets.

!omahaholdem 10000

Use exactly two of four hole cards and exactly three board cards across full betting streets.

!sevencardstud 10000

Receive hidden and exposed cards from third street through river with betting on every street.

!pineappleholdem 10000

Receive three hole cards, discard one after the flop, then continue through Hold’em streets.

!shortdeckholdem 10000

Play no-limit streets with a 36-card deck and short-deck hand ordering.

!badugi 10000

Complete four betting rounds and three draws to build the lowest four-suit, four-rank Badugi.

!highlowpoker 10000

Play seven-card stud and split the pot between the best high and qualifying eight-or-better low.

!indianpoker 10000

See the opponent’s card while your own stays hidden, then bet or fold under pressure.

!blackjacktable 10000

Each player chooses hit or stand against one shared dealer that draws to at least 17.

!baccarattable 10000

Choose Player, Banker, or Tie and resolve the hand with standard baccarat third-card rules.

!matgo 10000

Play two-player Matgo with 10-card hands, eight floor cards, matching, flipping, capture, Go, and Stop.

!gostop 10000

Play three-player Go-Stop with seven-card hands, six floor cards, matching, flipping, capture, Go, and Stop.

!onecard 10000

Play the existing turn-based One Card session with legal-card selection and ABADDON support.

!jokercatch 10000

Play the existing turn-based Joker Catch session with hidden-card choices and ABADDON support.
