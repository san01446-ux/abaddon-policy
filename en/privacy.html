Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-12

Data stored

  • Discord user and guild IDs
  • Gameplay progress, currency, items, quests, PvP and guild activity
  • Operational error and recovery records

Discord login

My Page uses the Discord OAuth identify scope. ABADDON does not collect Discord passwords. OAuth access tokens are not written into the gameplay save file, and website sessions expire.

Purpose

Data is used for persistent gameplay, rankings, My Page, service operations and recovery.

Deletion

Deletion requests can be submitted through the official Discord support channel. Backups may retain earlier records for a limited operational period.

Third parties

Optional features may connect to Discord OAuth, an AI provider, and Korean Discord List vote verification. Disabled integrations do not send data to those services.
